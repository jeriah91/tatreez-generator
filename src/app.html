Border pattern
Corner motif

Border modules frame the field one module deep and add to the composition’s size. Each side is split into square and double modules in a mirrored sequence; bands repeat from the centre outwards. Plant and tree bands stay upright on every side; geometric bands turn with the edge. Modules in mirror-image positions share a colourway, so the frame stays symmetric while all four colours stay balanced; where a large module makes that impossible without two touching modules sharing a background, each side is mirrored about its own centre instead. Click a border module to change the band; Shift-click to recolour the frame.

Large modules span 2×2 modules (28 × 28 stitches) or 2×4 (28 × 58) and carry complex charts close to their full size: whole medallions, tent squares and tall trees. They need a composition at least 2 modules wide and 2 high (4 high for the tallest).

Double orientation
Double-module content

Repeated motifs inside a tall or wide module sit 2 stitches apart — the same as the gutter between two modules — and are joined by a centre stem in the accent thread. Facing birds are left unjoined so they read as two birds.

Stitch

Colour

Every stitch meets at least 3:1 contrast against its background (WCAG 1.4.11), so stitches are always off-white on a colour, or a colour on off-white. Red/green, red/black and green/black are never layered. In densely stitched motifs, accent areas are left open rather than layered inside the field. Colours are balanced by total area across the composition, and touching modules never share a background.

Patterns

Region
Type

Orientation: every motif stays upright as it is stitched; cypress trees, plants, birds and vessels are never rotated or turned upside down. Motifs may only be mirrored left↔right.
Fill: every pattern spans its whole stitch field, touching all four sides, so the only empty space is the module’s 1-stitch margin.
Symmetry: every module is mirror-symmetric on at least one axis, colour included; motifs with no axis (birds, stepped corners) only appear as a mirrored facing pair. Tall and wide modules are always mirror-symmetric. Tall = a tall motif, or one upright motif repeated above itself. Wide = an upright motif and its mirror image facing each other.
Spacing: repeats are separated by a 2-stitch gap, equal to the gutter between modules; border repeats are evenly spaced.
Connection: the two repeats are joined by a stem along the centre line in the accent thread — 1 stitch wide inside each motif, 3 wide across the gap — stopping where it meets each motif.
